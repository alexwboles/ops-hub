## The honest connection

Four groups, one calm back office:

1. **Money** — InvoicePilot bills clients and nudges the late ones. CashFlow watches the 13-week horizon so a big payroll never lands the same week a customer pays late.
2. **People** — HireWise helps you write a fair, appealing job post and screen applicants on merit. OnboardPilot walks the new hire through Day 1, Week 1, and Day 30. ShiftPlan keeps the weekly schedule covered without the spreadsheet scramble.
3. **Paperwork** — SOPForge turns "how we do things here" into checklists. SignPilot gets quotes and work completions signed without per-envelope fees. BookPilot fills the appointment book while you work. TriagePilot keeps the owner's inbox sorted and drafted.
4. **Brain** — BizBrain holds the answers your team asks twice: policies, pricing, procedures — with sources — and logs the questions nobody has written down yet.

No single product pretends to do the others' job. They connect because money in, people on board, paperwork handled, and answers findable is a business that runs itself.
